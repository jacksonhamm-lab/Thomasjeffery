# Casual Menswear

The full wardrobe, not just the formal end. Knits, sport shirts, denim, trousers, outerwear, and more, from the brands worth knowing. In Calgary since 1968.

## What we carry

1. **Knitwear.** Ferrante and Codice for Italian-made merino. Robert Barakett for the luxury tee. Holebrook for casual layers.
2. **Sport shirts.** 7 Downie St. for golf and casual. Klauss Boehler and Blu by Polifroni for the polished end. DeSoto, Altemflower, and Codice round out the category. DeSoto reads well in business casual too.
3. **Denim.** 34 Heritage, designed with a higher rise and more room where most men need it. Alberto for European precision. Fidelity Denim.
4. **Trousers.** Non-denim casual and dress trousers from Alberto, Meyer, 34 Heritage, and Bugatti.
5. **Basics and essentials.** SAXX, Robert Barakett, Benchcraft, Marcoliani, and Pantherella.
6. **Outerwear.** Rotates by season. Bugatti's full outerwear range, including formal overcoats that carry into office wear, plus Holebrook and more.
