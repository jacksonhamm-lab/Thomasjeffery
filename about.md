# About Thomas Jeffery

## A Calgary store, with Calgary roots

Thomas Jeffery has been part of Calgary's menswear scene since 1968. The store carries the names of two sons from one of its earlier owners, Thomas and Jeffery. Today it is family owned and answerable to the city it has served for over half a century.

We carry the brands we believe in. We fit every piece in store. We don't sell what we wouldn't wear ourselves. The people who walk you through a fitting are usually the same ones you will see next time, often for years.

Tim Beggs and Jim Smith have each dedicated decades to this store. Alongside owner Len Hamm, they are the people behind the counter when you come in.

The store is at Kingsland Village. One location, more space under one roof.

## What makes us different

1. **Personal, not transactional.** We are not a chain. Every fitting happens with one staff member from start to finish. They learn your taste, your build, and what you actually wear.
2. **Honest opinions.** If a piece isn't right for you, we will tell you. If you don't need three suits, we won't sell you three.
3. **Tailoring in-house.** Our tailor works in the same building, on the same schedule we do. Nothing leaves the store until it fits the way it should.
4. **Brands we believe in.** Every brand on the floor earned its place because the construction holds up, the fit works for our customers, or it does something specific better than anyone else.

## The people

Many of the team have been here for decades, learning the craft from the people who came before them. They are experienced menswear professionals: the kind who can read a shoulder seam from across the room, and who often remember what you bought the last time you came in.
