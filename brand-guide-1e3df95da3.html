How we sound

Brand voice

So much menswear advertising uses the same polished language that it no longer stands out. We would rather give people a specific reason to come in.

Each line names something real: the product, the price, the date, or the place.

A good test is whether the line could run for any other store. If it could, it is worth making more specific.

Plain words and short sentences. It sounds more like the way our team talks in the store.

Words we leave out

elevate, curated, timeless, sophistication, impeccable, luxury, exclusive
They show up in most menswear ads, so they do not set us apart from any ecommerce brand or major retailer. We would rather show the thing they describe.
bespoke
Bespoke means a pattern drafted from scratch. Coppley custom is made to measure, so we keep the wording accurate.

The store dates to 1968, and our current owner has had it for part of that time. Family members work in the business alongside our sales team, so we keep the wording to locally owned.

Coppley makes the garment. We measure and fit it.

Coppley is the one brand we offer custom through.
Wedding groups
We coordinate the whole group, with fittings spread over a few visits.
Fit the whole group in one visit.
We would rather spread fittings out than rush anyone.
Tailoring
Alterations by our tailor on anything you buy here. We also alter clothing bought elsewhere, for a fee.
Advertising alterations as a service on their own. "Suit tailor near me."
Our focus is the clothing we sell. Outside alterations are charged and can take longer, because garments bought here come first.

Appointment only. By appointment.
Our door is open and walk-ins are welcome. Booking guarantees one-on-one time, which helps for weddings and custom orders.
