# Holiday 2026: Gifts for Him

Knitwear, shirts, scarves and gloves, sport coats, suits, gift certificates, and more, on the floor at Kingsland Village for the holiday season.

## The Holiday Gift Bonus, November 9 to December 24

Spend $250 or more, or buy a gift certificate, and get a bonus voucher for the next visit: $50 on $250, $100 on $500, $150 on $750, $200 on $1,000.

- Spend is before tax and includes sale items.
- Buying a gift certificate counts toward the spend.
- Amounts paid with a gift certificate or voucher don't count toward the spend.
- Bonus vouchers are for a future visit and can't be used on sale items. They have no expiry date.
- Gift certificates can be used on anything in the store, sale items included.
