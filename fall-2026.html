A note on the season

Fall is when menswear starts getting interesting. Warmer months thin everything out. Fall asks you to layer, and layering is where personal choice shows up. A knit under a sportcoat lands differently than the same knit over a shirt. The order of the pieces, the balance of texture, the weight of the fabric. All of it becomes the outfit.

Menswear right now is in a quieter place than it's been for a few years. Less streetwear crossover, more focus on the material. Knitwear paired with soft tailoring. Sportcoats worn the way you'd wear a sweater. Fabrics that read from across a room. Donegal, boiled wool, chunky merino, cashmere blends. The brands leaning into this are the ones with real craft behind them, and that's most of what we carry.

— The Thomas Jeffery Team
