# Brands We Carry

Every brand on the floor was chosen for a reason.

## Featured

- **Coppley** — Hamilton, Canada, est. 1883. One of North America's oldest tailoring houses and one of the last makers still doing genuine half-canvas construction. We carry Coppley ready-to-wear and offer their full custom program, made to your exact measurements in Canada.
- **Paul Betenly** — est. 1981, fabric designed in Milan. Hand-sewn armholes, pick stitching throughout, and performance fabrics across the line. Vertically integrated from their own sheep farms in Australia and New Zealand through their own yarn, fabric, and factory. Our default recommendation for wedding parties and first suits.
- **Jack Victor** — Montreal, Canada, est. 1913. Fabrics selected in Europe, cut and sewn in Montreal. Their sport jackets have been a Thomas Jeffery staple for years.
- **Bugatti** — Germany, est. 1947, still run by the Brinkmann family. We carry Bugatti's broadest range in Calgary: trousers, outerwear, knitwear, sport shirts, accessories, and more.
- **7 Downie St.** — Canada. Built around golf, weekends, and travel. Sport shirts, casual knits, light layers, and sport jackets.
- **Alberto** — Mönchengladbach, Germany, est. 1922. A trouser house with no diversification into other categories. Each pair is built from 36 components through roughly 40 production steps.
