- **Phone:** (403) 278-2445

## What brings you in

- **[Office](https://thomasjeffery.ca/office)** — suits, sport coats, dress shirts, and trousers. Sharp every day, built to last.
- **[Wedding](https://thomasjeffery.ca/wedding)** — from the groom to the last groomsman.
- **[Casual](https://thomasjeffery.ca/casual)** — knits, sport shirts, denim, outerwear, and more. Well-made pieces worth keeping.

## How we work

Every fitting happens with one staff member from start to finish. Alterations are handled in-house by our own tailor, in the same building. Walk-ins are welcome any day; Saturdays fill up fast, so book ahead.
